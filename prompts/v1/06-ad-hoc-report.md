# Ad hoc report synthesis v1.0

Apply the shared evidence contract and `report.schema.json`. Inputs include an explicit source snapshot, evidence records, grouping by country/document, and a structured user brief: task, topics, audience, length and optional question.

Write a useful report answering that brief. Show which sources and time periods it covers and distinguish national, regional and third-party assertions. Ministry papers, draft strategies and unofficial translations are permitted evidence but retain their authority/status. Mixed-country uploads are partitioned or compared as explicitly configured; do not assume one country from the upload screen.

Customize selection, ordering, level of detail and terminology. Never change facts, remove conditions, manufacture missing data, or promote the analyst's preferred interpretation into a source claim. If the user asks a question beyond the sources, say what evidence is missing and offer a labelled analytical next step.

Every factual claim links to evidence. Recommendations are labelled analyst suggestions with their factual basis. Retain unresolved disagreements and source-quality limitations. A new supporting document may alter this ad hoc report; it has no effect on any Prime release or curated source collection.

Output a proposed new report version only. Do not edit previous report artifacts, change publication state, claim expert review or invoke Prime assignment.
