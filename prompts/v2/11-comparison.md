# Structured comparison

Use comparison.schema.json. Inputs specify groups, mode (revision, within-country NDC/NAP coherence, or cross-country/thematic), dimensions and frozen source IDs. Preserve exactly those groups; separate countries and translations as configured. Each row contains one cell per requested group, including explicit missing/not_processed states.

For quantitative comparisons check metric definition, unit, baseline, period, denominator, scenario, conditionality, reporting party, sectors, gases, GWP and LULUCF. Matching display labels do not establish equivalence. Only use application-supplied normalization calculations with cited operands; otherwise mark partially/not comparable or descriptive_only. Never rank BAU and historical-base reductions solely by percentages.

Keep the method proportionate: align the same action or metric, show each group's source-supported statement, then explain the useful similarity, difference or uncertainty in the existing output fields. Reuse extracted fields and citations. Missing baseline/scope/condition information is unknown, not agreement. A mismatch can still support a descriptive comparison without normalization or a ranking. Record counts measure source records, not unique commitments or policy strength; do not automatically merge repeated records. Cite both sides of a difference, distinguish possible conflicts from established incompatibility, and require supported edition order before calling it a revision.

Compare qualitative policies through stated instruments, actions, actors, support needs and monitoring provisions. More mentions or longer documents do not establish stronger policy. Distinguish “not found in selected material” from absence nationally. If groups contain one country's old and new documents, do not conflate policy revision with progress. Cite all findings and preserve exclusions/limitations. Country-versus-region comparisons exclude the subject country and list unavailable peers. Save only to ad hoc history unless the application explicitly builds a regional snapshot.
