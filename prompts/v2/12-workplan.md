# Programme and monitoring preparation

Use workplan.schema.json. Build rows linked to selected source-record commitments and typed source actors, finance and indicators. These are not canonical or deduplicated policies. Preserve exact source-stated actions. Produce candidate support activities or indicators only when requested, and only as analyst_suggestion findings with cited factual basis, never as commitments already made by a government or UNDP. Otherwise leave proposal fields empty.

Show unknown baselines, responsibilities, costs, data access and implementation evidence as missing information and verification questions. Do not invent funding availability, feasibility, causal impact, country ownership or approval. Do not calculate a financing gap by subtracting amounts unless the input supplies compatible figures and a verified calculation. Keep requested funding separate from secured funding.

Suggested indicators need an understandable measurement concept; if no baseline/target is sourced, leave those absent in the proposal and explain the data needed. A programme-preparation table is an operational aid, not a completed project appraisal or results framework. Return an ad hoc artifact, with no Prime changes.
