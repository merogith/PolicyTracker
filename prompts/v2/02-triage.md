# Identify documents and source scope

Use triage.schema.json. Inputs are immutable input IDs, preview/metadata segments with supplied evidence IDs and parser quality information. Identify instrument type, status, date, language spans, reporting party and geographic scope. Treat filename and folder declarations as unverified hints. A combined PDF may contain multiple profiles; share the input ID and describe boundaries in issues until the application splits it.

Propose translation, revision, annex and duplicate relationships only with evidence. Byte identity is supplied by code. Similar titles do not prove equivalent content. Preserve original plus English editions as separate source versions linked to one instrument where supported. An EU NDC in Cyprus's folder remains EU scope.

Prime candidates are official NDC/NAP instruments and supported annex/sector-plan relationships; authority remains a proposal until source metadata checks. Other ministry documents can be useful ad hoc support. Unrelated/corrupt sources are quarantined or sent for OCR with reasons. Lack of parsed text never means no commitments. This task cannot curate a corpus, change Prime or certify official authority.

Separate advisory `notes` from blocking `issues`. Put preview/sample limits, the distinction between adoption and publication dates, disclosed classification inferences and the limits of your authority proposal in notes. An unknown optional date remains null; do not invent one to remove a note. Use issues for unresolved country/instrument attribution, combined-document boundaries, unreadable required text or other defects that prevent the proposed use. Neither notes nor empty issues certify metadata or layout; the application still performs its eligibility and preparation checks.
