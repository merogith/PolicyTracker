# Labeling rules — lean method 3

Use the supplied versioned taxonomy, vocabulary and labeling guide. Definitions explain categories; they are not evidence that a category applies. Original wording and citations remain authoritative.

## One claim, one statement type

Use `kind` for the single statement type: context, priority, proposal, commitment, implementation_observation, result_observation, projection, scenario or unclear. There is no additional assertion kind or documentation status. An attributed undertaking can be qualitative or conditional. A recommendation or draft proposal without established adoption is not a government commitment. Preserve source status and reporting party.

Separate independently actionable measures and separate promises from reports of delivery or outcomes. One integrated intervention with several attributes stays together. A commitment with explicit priority wording stays a commitment; preserve that wording in its description/details rather than duplicating the record. Historical statements keep their substantive type and reference period. Blank templates belong in the coverage ledger, not invented country records.

Distinguish installations and programme activity (implementation) from reported outcomes (results). A projection using a scenario is a projection with scenario details. Intended future deliverables are not observed results. Preserve negation: “no warning system operates” is a contextual finding about absence, not implementation. A passed deadline does not establish failure.

## Topics, domains and optional labels

Domain → topic is the only hierarchy. Select the narrowest substantively supported topic IDs; the application derives domains from their union. Do not independently assign a primary domain, duplicate records per sector, or create Topic 1/2/3 columns. Multiple topics and facets are allowed with evidence for each. Irrelevant lists stay empty. Preserve unmapped terms with their original wording, evidence and proposed meaning; do not force a default Climate topic or invent permanent IDs.

Topic answers the subject; objective answers policy purpose; hazard answers the threat; instrument answers the mechanism; group answers whom the source addresses. Substantive themes remain optional. A funding amount alone does not require a Finance theme or climate-finance topic. A group mention alone does not establish gender/disability analysis or a benefit. Preserve affected, targeted and participating roles in linked details.

An irrigation intervention may map to Agriculture and Water through AGR-01. Solar electricity can additionally support ENE-01 when substantive. Urban taxonomy membership is not urban geographic scope. Climate & disaster policy is a cross-sector navigation grouping, not evidence of climate causation. Earthquake evidence is not automatically adaptation.

Select objectives only with wording or identified context. Climate mitigation means emissions reduction or carbon sinks; “flood mitigation” alone supports neither that label nor inferred climate causation. Adaptation and DRR may both apply where evidenced. Source location in a NAP alone is insufficient. This meaning distinction follows UNDRR terminology; our IDs remain project choices.

## One risk and resilience function pool

Use `risk_functions`: risk_understanding, risk_prevention_reduction, early_warning_preparedness, anticipatory_action, emergency_response, recovery_reconstruction, coping_continuity and systemic_transformation. Select every specific supported function. Do not add generic cross-phase, adapt, anticipate or unspecified labels. General resilience wording may leave functions empty.

Readiness and warnings do not by themselves establish forecast-triggered anticipatory action. Maintaining services during disruption differs from rebuilding afterward. Routine improvements do not establish systemic transformation. Hazard context alone does not establish an action function or an achieved reduction in risk.

Co-occurring labels are finding aids, not every possible hazard–function relationship. Split distinct heat-preparedness and flood-recovery actions. If the programme is inseparable, preserve the specific links in risk_contexts/details and explain their scope. Risk components are optional analytical details; do not repeat every populated risk field as a required labeling exercise. Keep explicitly useful residual/cascading risk distinctions.

## Facts, variants and limitations

Keep target baselines, units, deadlines, scope and conditions together. Attach actors, finance, indicators and risk details to the target IDs they qualify. Shared record-level information does not become an allocation to every sector or scenario. Keep unconditional and conditional variants distinct without adding or averaging them.

Use existing missing_fields only where a missing value changes interpretation. “Not stated” requires adequately reviewed relevant evidence. Unreadable/unprocessed material belongs in coverage and issues; never convert it to source absence. Preserve conflicting values and source relationships. Missing reasons are evidence metadata, not policy labels. Empty fields and arrays do not prove national absence.

Each substantive label needs field_support identifying the supporting evidence and explicit/inherited/classification basis. Do not assign numerical confidence scores. Derived displays do not add source claims. Count each source record once overall and once per applicable category; category totals overlap. Repeated source statements remain separate records with proposed links, not deduplicated policy counts. Future relabeling creates a new method version and never rewrites archived facts.
