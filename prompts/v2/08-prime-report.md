# Country Profile (internal route: prime)

Use report.schema.json. The input contains one frozen curated country corpus, validated source records, typed linked details, proposed relationships/change findings, English/source coverage and required section IDs. Produce all required sections with explicit found/not_found_in_scope/not_applicable/not_processed states. Prepare one coherent Country Profile. The application presents two core views: Overview and Policy register. They have one release ID and one published-version selection. Documents & translations and Version history are supporting utilities. One eligible document is sufficient to generate a useful profile; disclose its scope and missing instruments without inventing content.

Write a five-minute brief, then full sections on adaptation/DRR, mitigation, actors/implementation, finance/support, inclusion/development, monitoring, changes/coherence and limitations. Every factual finding cites known record/evidence IDs. Explain priorities using explicit source priority, scale, deadline or relevance; do not invent a composite score. The complete register is a separate deterministic view; do not repeat every row in prose.

Check both citation lists for every factual, observed or derived finding: record_ids and evidence_ids must both be nonempty, and every cited evidence ID must belong to at least one of that finding's cited records. An evidence quote without a supporting record cannot establish a standalone finding. Preserve parser, translation and scope qualifications in the report's limitations instead; never invent a record link, drop a qualification, or relabel a factual statement as an analyst suggestion to pass validation.

Overview uses headline_findings, prioritized_commitments and the expandable thematic sections. Keep implementation, finance, inclusion, monitoring and risk/DRR findings explicit. The Policy register presents the unchanged source records and typed details, with sector, risk/DRR and delivery filters; do not create a second prose report for it. Count source records only when useful; never present record or proposed-group counts as unique commitments. Repeated statements can be summarized together with citations to each source without merging their records. changes_and_coherence is a conditional subsection of the full narrative, not a required standalone tool. With one document, mark cross-document alignment/revisions not_applicable and explain why. Internal tensions may be described when evidenced, without inventing change over time. With suitable pairs, explain within-country links and changes. Regional rankings and custom comparisons belong to separate Analysis tools. Not restated does not by itself mean revoked; a translation is not a policy revision.

Treat reconciliation as proposed analysis, not an established policy fact. Carry unresolved differences into the narrative when relevant and cite both sides. Describe qualitative similarities/differences directly. Before a numerical comparison, check the metric, unit, baseline/denominator, period, scope and conditions/scenario; explain any mismatch instead of calculating a change. Missing comparability information stays unknown. A numerical change is not by itself stronger ambition or implementation progress. Before/after wording requires supported edition order and continuity; otherwise describe differences between sources. Do not build a separate comparison process for this subsection.

Include a dedicated risk_context_and_drr section covering documented hazards, exposure, vulnerability, capacity and DRR actions across prevention, preparedness, response and recovery. Distinguish the country's reported risk context from our evidence availability. Do not equate an NDC/NAP source collection with a complete national disaster-risk assessment. Highlight risk-informed planning connections only when the evidence supports them.

Highlight conditionality beside headline numbers. Keep current/historical/parallel variants distinguishable. Trace institutions and support needs to the appropriate actions. “Not stated” is a documentation gap, not proof that capacity or funding is absent. Source-reported implementation stays dated and attributed. NDC/NAP-only evidence generally cannot establish actual progress.

Follow-up questions are analyst_suggestion findings grounded in evidence. For partial scope, specify missing material and restrict conclusions. No documents means an availability response and no factual country narrative. The application publishes successful eligible outputs automatically unless a different Prime is pinned; do not claim publication yourself or request human approval.

Section state applies to the entire section: a section containing supported internal tensions is found even if cross-document comparison is not applicable. Explain that comparison limitation in the findings/limitations; do not attach factual findings to a not_applicable section. Never use not_found_in_scope when relevant material remains unreadable or unprocessed; use not_processed and partial availability. A source-supported absence statement is a cited finding, distinct from failing to find evidence.
